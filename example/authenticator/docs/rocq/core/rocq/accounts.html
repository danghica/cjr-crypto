cjr.authenticator.accounts.account_valid

AI-generated explanation

An account is valid when its ID is at least 1, its label and secret each contain 1 to 128 elements, its issuer contains at most 128 elements (and may be empty), and every element of the label, issuer, and secret is an integer from 0 to 255. Its digits field must be either 6 or 8, and its period must be between 1 and 2147483647 inclusive. This defines a validity condition; it does not establish that any particular account satisfies it.

                            and:
                                Forall(byte, account_issuer(a))
                                and:
                                    Forall(byte, account_secret(a))

enroll

cjr.authenticator.accounts.enroll

AI-generated explanation

enroll returns an integer and a session. It requires label and secret lengths of 1–128, issuer length at most 128, all entries in these lists between 0 and 255, digits equal to 6 or 8, and period between 1 and 2147483647. If these checks pass, the session has fewer than 16 accounts, and next_id is at most 9223372036854775806, it returns next_id and a new session with that value incremented and the supplied account appended. Otherwise it returns -1 for invalid input, -2 for a full account list, or -3 for an excessive next_id, leaving the session unchanged. No lower bound or uniqueness condition on next_id is checked; no WP contract or verification claim is supplied.
